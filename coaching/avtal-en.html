Coaching agreement & alliance

Here I describe how I work with coaching agreements: a formal agreement with the Sponsor that sets the frame, budget and mandate, and an Alliance with the Team/Client that builds the real relationship around sub-goals and buy-in. The templates below can be filled in directly on this page, and downloaded as a PDF once you're done.

Coaching Agreement (with the Sponsor)

The first conversation, with whoever commissions or sponsors the coaching. Sets the formal frame.

Goals & success criteria

What should the coaching lead to, and how will we know it worked?

Timeframe

How long does the engagement run, and when do we check in?

Frames & mandate

What mandate do I have as coach, and where are the boundaries?

Budget & scope

What's included, and what does it cost?

Boundaries

What's explicitly not included in this engagement?

Confidentiality

What the sponsor can and can't know about what happens in the coaching.

Feedback

How and when the sponsor gets updates, without breaking the client's confidentiality.

Availability

The practical conditions I need to be able to deliver.

Alliance (with Team/Client)

The deeper conversation, with the team or individual actually being coached. Builds the real relationship.

Sub-goals

Concrete steps that feel meaningful to them, not just the sponsor's goal.

Agreement to try

A genuine, stated yes, not silent acceptance.

Buy-in

What shows they actually want this?

Conditions

What needs to be true for this to actually work for them?

Known risks & resistance

Name openly what could get in the way: time pressure, bad past experiences, reluctance.

Confidentiality

Reaffirmed from their perspective: what stays with me.

Availability

Do they actually have the time and space to engage?
