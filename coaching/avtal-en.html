Coaching agreement & alliance

Here I describe how I set up a coaching agreement, including any three-way agreement with the coachee and the client organization, and how I establish a clear alliance around goals, roles and ground rules before the coaching work itself begins.

Below is a first draft of the two templates. I'll go through them with my course leader for feedback before they're finished.

Coaching Agreement (with the Sponsor)

The first conversation, with whoever commissions or sponsors the coaching. Sets the formal frame.

  • 01
    Goals & success criteriaWhat should the coaching lead to, and how will we know it worked?
  • 02
    TimeframeHow long does the engagement run, and when do we check in?
  • 03
    Frames & mandateWhat mandate do I have as coach, and where are the boundaries?
  • 04
    Budget & scopeWhat's included, and what does it cost?
  • 05
    BoundariesWhat's explicitly not included in this engagement?
  • 06
    ConfidentialityWhat the sponsor can and can't know about what happens in the coaching.
  • 07
    FeedbackHow and when the sponsor gets updates, without breaking the client's confidentiality.
  • 08
    AvailabilityThe practical conditions I need to be able to deliver.

Alliance (with Team/Client)

The deeper conversation, with the team or individual actually being coached. Builds the real relationship.

  • 01
    Sub-goalsConcrete steps that feel meaningful to them, not just the sponsor's goal.
  • 02
    Agreement to tryA genuine, stated yes, not silent acceptance.
  • 03
    Buy-inWhat shows they actually want this?
  • 04
    ConditionsWhat needs to be true for this to actually work for them?
  • 05
    Known risks & resistanceName openly what could get in the way: time pressure, bad past experiences, reluctance.
  • 06
    ConfidentialityReaffirmed from their perspective: what stays with me.
  • 07
    AvailabilityDo they actually have the time and space to engage?
